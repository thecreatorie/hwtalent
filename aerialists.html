Questions

Aerialists, asked often.

Do you bring the rigging?

Yes. Our aerialists perform on our own freestanding rigs, from 8 to 20 feet tall, installed by our crew. If your venue has its own rigging point, tell us and your director will confirm it.

How high does the ceiling need to be?

From about 8 feet for our smallest rig. More height means more of a show. Tell us your ceiling and we will tell you what works.

Can aerialists perform outdoors?

Yes. Our freestanding rigs stand indoors and out.

Can we book one aerialist, or several?

One artist or a full cast, the standard is the same.

Can the costume match our theme?

Yes. Costumes are designed for your event and made by hand, in your colours.

Do you travel?

Yes. We are based in Las Vegas and regularly work in California, Arizona and Texas. Anywhere else in the country, ask.
