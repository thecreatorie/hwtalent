Questions

Asked often.

Can I book a single performer?

Yes. One artist or a full cast, the standard is the same.

I do not see what I am imagining.

Good. This list is where we start, not where we stop.

Can acts be combined?

That is where it gets interesting. Fire in the air. Light on stilts. Ask for the combination nobody has seen.

Who are the performers?

Licensed, vetted professionals, chosen by us for each night. Nobody is sent from a list.

How involved do we need to be?

As hands-on or as hands-off as you like. Approve every stitch, or hand us the night and arrive as a guest.

Do you travel?

Yes. We are based in Las Vegas and regularly work in California, Arizona and Texas. Anywhere else in the country, ask.
