      Questions

      Asked often.

      What is included?

      Delivery, installation, lighting, professional decor and strike, all by our own crew.

      Can it match our colours or theme?

      Yes. Name them in the booking and the lighting and decor are styled to it.

      How is the figure set?

      By the pieces, the length of the rental and the place. You see it before you commit to anything.

      How long can we keep it?

      From one day to thirty. The longer the stay, the gentler the daily rate.

      How do we reserve?

      Sign the agreement on this page. Your date is held with the retainer.

      What do you need from the venue?

      A ceiling height and a clear floor. We tell you what works.

      Which sizes and lengths?

      Lyras at 34, 36 or 38 inches. Silks, hammocks and chains hung at the length you ask for. Tell us in the booking.

      Outside Las Vegas?

      California, Arizona, Texas and beyond. Your director arranges travel and freight.
