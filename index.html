Questions

Asked often.

What can you actually do?

Almost anything a body, a flame or a light can do. If it has never been done, even better.

How does it begin?

With thirty minutes. Your Entertainment Director learns the event you are imagining. Then we design it, cast it, build it and run it.

Who is the Entertainment Director?

One person, dedicated to your event, who holds every detail from the first call to the final bow. Nothing is lost in translation.

How involved do we need to be?

As hands-on or as hands-off as you like. Approve every stitch, or hand us the event and arrive as a guest.

How far ahead should we talk?

The earlier, the wilder. Costumes are made by hand and shows are built from nothing. Short notice is still worth a call.

Is fire possible at our venue?

Usually. Our fire artists are licensed, vetted professionals, and we arrange it with your venue directly.

Can it carry our brand?

Yes. Your logo on the costumes, on the props, and in the light itself.

Do you travel?

Yes. We are based in Las Vegas and regularly work in California, Arizona and Texas. Anywhere else in the country, ask.
